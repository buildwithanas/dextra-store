Publish your changes

This dashboard saves a draft in your browser. The live shop only changes when you replace its files.

  1. Click Download data.js.
  2. Put it in the public folder, replacing the old data.js.
  3. Copy any downloaded product photos into public/images/.
  4. Upload the public folder to your host again.

Because nothing goes live until you upload it, visitors cannot change your shop through this page. Only upload the public folder. Keep the admin folder on your computer.
